# Study scope and interpretation

The method analyzes a finite set of paths whose model score falls within a specified deficit of the best path. Classical Lagrangian support queries produce outer bounds on a downstream confidence functional. Explicit feasible paths provide inner witnesses. Projected frame and segment controls provide comparisons, and the reported functional intervals include the specified segment intersection.

A stable score sign means that all paths in the stated model-defined set have the same thresholded site-score sign. It is not a population confidence interval, a complete multi-site referral guarantee, or evidence that the chosen path is physiologically correct. Different murmur topology winners may remain attainable inside a stable-sign condition.

The initial 24 and additional 48 participants are disjoint, but both samples are consumed development data. The 48-person breadth sample is reused across eight predictors and two budgets. Seeds, models, budgets and records do not increase the independent participant count.

The positive comparison is against the specified frame/segment projection controls. A stronger complete or completeness-aware global constrained-path comparator, the complete referral policy, unused external evaluation, and clinical explanation validation remain outstanding.

The support-gap distillation trials were falsification experiments. The fresh trial compared emission KL, nominal-score matching and support-gap matching over three seeds. The adaptive warm-start trial added nominal-confidence matching, retaining all four arms over the same three seeds. Both predeclared engineering criteria failed. All 21 final fits remain in `analysis/evidence/distillation_all_21_models.csv`.

The breadth partition is 701 resolved conditions, 48 unresolved conditions with feasible paths on both sides, and 19 remaining undetermined conditions. An earlier narrative said 20 in the last category; the released evidence and article use the recomputed value 19. See `analysis/evidence/CORRECTION.txt`.

The algorithms use float64 arithmetic, explicit numerical tolerances and the legacy boundary/floor convention documented in the protocols. These numerical checks are not a formal proof about real arithmetic or a new optimization method.
