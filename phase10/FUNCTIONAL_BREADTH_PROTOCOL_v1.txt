FUNCTIONAL-BOUND BREADTH CHECK — frozen before new posterior-array reads
8 November 2026

Purpose
Assess the useful non-learning decoder result beyond the initial 24 people and
one model/budget. This is an internal development extension, not external
validation or pristine clinical confirmation. All pilot_validation participants
were already part of phase9 development. No disease labels or phase annotations
are needed. Do not use final_corroboration_v1 or reserved_test arrays.

Selection
From identifier/site/role/selected inventory columns, exclude the 24 group IDs
in the original phase10 selection. Restrict to pilot_validation and selected
AV/PV/TV/MV records. Choose one record per linked person by this site order then
lexical record. Order remaining groups by SHA256('phase10-breadth-v1:'+group_id)
and take 48. Seal selection, all record-array references and source hashes
before reading the new arrays. No replacement based on predictions or labels.

Sixteen repeated conditions on the same 48 people
Models: float and INT8 teacher ensembles from hsmm_int8_control_v1; short
physiological students with alpha=0 and alpha=1 for seeds 20261107, 20261108,
20261109. Budgets: 20 seconds and full recordings. Thus 8 models * 2 budgets,
768 record-condition evaluations, but only 48 distinct people. Keep conditions
separate; do not count repeated conditions as independent patients.

Fixed calculations
All four topologies, eta=.005, unchanged float64 legacy-boundary DAG, original
potential construction, adaptive dual bisection 14 iterations. Comparators:
frame-state projection; segment/transition-edge projection; functional bounds;
their intersection with the segment bounds; fixed queries {-1,+1} with simple
row-wise confidence limits and no learned parameters. Retain every comparator.
Measure native site-score sign resolution, interval widths and constructive
both-sign paths. Report possible/witnessed winning topologies and stable-sign
cases with multiple witnessed winners. Neither score sign nor winner certifies
disease correctness, true physiology, audio robustness or referral safety.

Audit and gate
Verify each selected array and raw-posterior hash against its frozen source
ledger. Reconstruct every feasible lower/upper witness directly from segments
and emissions, check deficit feasibility, interval containment and saved-path
confidence. Retain witnesses and their hashes. Missing/invalid arrays are
visible failures with no replacement; the engineering gate fails if any occur.
Report all denominators and every one of the 16 conditions.

Pass the descriptive breadth gate only if at least 12/16 conditions have >=50%
median paired width reduction for intersected functional/segment bounds versus
segment projection, plus at least five additional resolved signs among 48 in
that condition. This is NOT a statistical significance criterion or evidence
that generic Lagrangian bounds are new. No clinical accuracy analysis or model
selection. Parameterized graph semantics and numerical-guard limitations from
PROTOCOL_v1.txt remain in force. Preserve the failed distillation experiments.
