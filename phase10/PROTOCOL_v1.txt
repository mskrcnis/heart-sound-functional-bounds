Phase 10: decision functionals of ambiguous physiological paths
Recorded 2026-11-08 BEFORE the new diagnostic results.

Purpose
Test a narrow candidate: efficient outer bounds and feasible witnesses for a
downstream additive screening score over globally near-optimal HSMM paths.
This is an exploratory method feasibility study, not a novelty declaration or
clinical superiority test. The consumed phase9 reserve is prohibited here.

Known prior art
Nicosia (arXiv:2606.17181v1, June 2026) already defines near-optimal path tubes,
exact local projections, stability gaps and score-perturbation guarantees.
Brown and Golod (2010) already explore k-best alternative explanations.
Maua et al. (2014) already perform imprecise-HMM sensitivity analysis.
Lagrangian relaxation of resource-constrained paths is classical. None of
these ingredients, nor prefix-sum acceleration, is claimed as our invention.
The narrower question is whether decision-functional bounds offer measurable
value beyond local projected uncertainty, and eventually a useful target for
compact decision-aware distillation. Training is conditional on this gate.

Mathematical object
For fixed posterior p and fixed duration/transition potentials, each topology
has a finite acyclic path graph. L(q) is its additive log score; R(q) is the sum
of selected emission probabilities, counted once per observed frame. Let
L*=max_q L(q), Q_e={q:L(q)>=L*-e}. We seek the range of C(q)=R(q)/T on Q_e.
For any lambda>0,
  max_Qe C <= [max_q{L(q)+lambda R(q)}-L*+e]/(lambda T).
For lambda<0 the same expression is a lower bound on min_Qe C.
These inequalities follow immediately from weak duality. Returned feasible
paths separately give inner bounds. No claim of zero duality gap is made.
Intersect the dual outer bounds with the outer bounds from exact frame-state
projections. Use all ties, never treat the projection Cartesian product as a
set of feasible complete paths.

Each of Healthy/Holo/Early/Mid has its own independent likelihood tolerance.
For score s=max_m C_m-C_H, a sound interval is
  [max_m lower_m-upper_H, max_m upper_m-lower_H].
Feasible per-topology paths give constructive score witnesses. This is a
fixed-posterior, fixed-duration diagnostic, not robustness to audio changes,
parameter uncertainty, true-state coverage or disease correctness. The
quality rule and visit aggregation are NOT evaluated in this first record
diagnostic. Sign(s) is a native site-score diagnostic, NOT the deployed policy.

Implementation contract
Represent the corrected legacy decoder's boundary graph, including its
duplicate first-emission initialization, endpoint clamping, FLT_MIN transition
and duration floors, and terminal extension. Use float64 additive arithmetic
and prefix sums; do not claim bitwise equivalence to legacy float32 summation.
Compare zero-tilt paths/confidences against saved legacy outputs and report
every mismatch. Never change frozen phase9 code, weights, reports or results.
Validate the graph and bounds independently by exhaustive enumeration of
small synthetic instances, including exact ties and terminal boundaries.
Numerical guard: 1e-8 in total log score and 1e-10 in confidence. These are
engineering tolerances, not outward-rounded machine-verified certificates.

Data selection BEFORE array inspection
Use only already-saved pilot_validation posteriors from the fixed short
pure-KD model seed 20261107, alpha=1, at 20 seconds/site. Verify original
array hashes. Read only PatientID/group_id/role from split metadata.
Choose one record per linked person: first canonical available site in
AV,PV,TV,MV order, then lexical record. Sort persons by SHA256 of
'phase10-v1:' + group_id and take the first 24. No labels or phase annotations
are loaded for selection or evaluation. Save the selection manifest before
opening selected arrays. All 24 records and all four topologies are retained.

Fixed diagnostic grid and comparisons
Normalized deficit eta=e/T in [0,0.001,0.005,0.01,0.05]. These are sensitivity
scales, not calibrated probability levels. Compare: (1) single Viterbi score;
(2) exact projected-state outer bounds; (3) projection intersected with dual
outer bounds, plus feasible witnesses. For each topology and tolerance report
range width, witness/outer gaps, frame ambiguity, runtime and solver calls.
For each record report score width, sign certification, and whether feasible
paths actually attain both signs. Include runtime excluding JIT compilation;
this is method diagnostic timing, not a rural-device deployment benchmark.

Go/no-go
Correctness is mandatory: no exhaustive-enumeration containment failures,
projection errors or invalid feasible witnesses. Report numerical mismatches.
Proceed to a training objective only if decision-functional bounds materially
tighten projected bounds (median width reduction >=25% at eta=0.005) and
resolve at least two additional site signs among the fixed 24 records at any
declared nonzero eta, or constructively demonstrate both signs in >=2 records.
These are engineering feasibility criteria, not inferential significance or
evidence of novelty. Do not change them after seeing outcomes.
Novelty remains a separate full-text review gate. If the implementation is
only a standard relaxation applied to this task, describe it that way.
