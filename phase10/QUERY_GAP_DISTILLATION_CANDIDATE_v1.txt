Candidate generated from phase10 findings, 2026-11-08.
Status: mathematical formulation and differentiable implementation prototype;
not yet a trained-model result, novelty claim, or clinical guarantee.

Question
Can a small physiological student preserve a teacher's decision-uncertainty
curve while being allowed to disagree on unidentifiable latent paths?
The pilot makes this question concrete: at eta=.005, 12/24 records have a
resolved site-score sign yet constructive competing winning topologies.

Proposed transfer target
For a fixed input, topology m, model theta, and additive reward R_theta(q), let
  V_theta(lambda)=max_q [L_theta(q)+lambda R_theta(q)],
  G_theta(lambda)=[V_theta(lambda)-V_theta(0)]/(lambda T), lambda!=0.
For a fixed finite set Lambda with positive and negative values, define
  upper_theta(eta)=min_{lambda>0} [G_theta(lambda)+eta/lambda],
  lower_theta(eta)=max_{lambda<0} [G_theta(lambda)+eta/lambda].
These are weak-duality outer bounds on R/T for paths with log-score loss at
most eta*T. They are valid without a projection pass. The confidence reward
uses all frames; fixed-window rewards use zero weights outside the window.

Candidate loss
Match G_student to G_teacher on topology/reward/query tuples, alongside a
declared ordinary emission-KL control. A starting query set is {-1,+1}; it is
motivated by the successful untrained bound baseline, not tuned on outcomes.
Use an L1 or smooth-L1 discrepancy with fixed weights. A new loss name does
not establish originality. Compare against ordinary KL, hard-state learning,
nominal-score-only matching and structured marginal KD before scaling.

Pointwise transfer property (elementary consequence, not claimed new theorem)
If max_{lambda in Lambda}|G_S(lambda)-G_T(lambda)|<=delta for an input, then
the finite-query lower and upper envelopes each differ by <=delta for EVERY
eta>=0. Proof: the eta/lambda terms are identical, and min/max are 1-Lipschitz
in the sup norm. For max-murmur confidence minus healthy confidence, separate
topology error bounds imply a <=delta_M+delta_H difference for each endpoint.
This property concerns these finite-query OUTER bounds, not exact extrema,
true physiological states, disease correctness or unseen-input guarantees.
It does not cover extra student-specific clipping/projection operations unless
those operations are separately controlled. A low mean training loss does
not establish the assumed pointwise error bound on future inputs.

Why it is a different target from ordinary KD
Emission KL matches local distributions; nominal-score matching transfers one
chosen pair of paths. This target matches changes in globally optimized path
values under a task-specific additive tilt, thereby controlling a whole family
of finite-query uncertainty bounds. Student and teacher paths need not agree.
It remains closely related to energy matching, structured KD, decision-focused
compression and uncertainty distillation. Those comparisons must be resolved
before describing the formulation as novel.

Implementation boundary
The prototype treats duration and transition potentials as FIXED inputs. It
differentiates the selected support paths using the usual max-envelope rule.
At ties it returns a selected subgradient, not a unique derivative. Non-smooth
differences of maxima are not convex in network parameters. Existing heart-
rate selection and duration truncation are discrete; they cannot silently be
treated as differentiable. Full-record training and recomputed-duration
inference need an explicit consistent design before an experiment.

Gate before any full model campaign
1. Verify values against the independent DAG implementation and gradients by
   finite differences away from ties, including float32 input and CUDA smoke.
2. Read the closest structured uncertainty/energy/credal distillation methods.
3. Freeze a bounded train/development comparison that includes the simple
   controls. Use continuous training recordings; do not decode the existing
   gap-collapsed training cache as though it were uninterrupted physiology.
4. Retain all old negative results. No phase9 reserve reuse as confirmation.
