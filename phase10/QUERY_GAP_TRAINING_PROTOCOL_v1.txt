BOUNDED SUPPORT-GAP DISTILLATION PILOT — frozen before waveform reads/fitting
8 November 2026

Purpose and status
Test whether a particular structured functional target improves out-of-fit
teacher-bound fidelity beyond ordinary emission KL and nominal-score matching.
This is adaptive development after the 24-record decoder study. It cannot
establish disease superiority, independent confirmation, or original invention.
The previous phase9 final reserve and manuscripts remain frozen. Generic weak
duality, max-envelope gradients and uncertainty distillation are prior art.

Selection and continuous input
Read only identifier, site, selected, group and role columns of the original
inventory. Within train, choose one selected AV/PV/TV/MV record per linked
person by this site order, then lexical record order. Order people by SHA256
of 'phase10-querygap-v1:' + group_id. Take the first 96: first 80 fit, last 16
monitor. No label or annotation criterion. Development uses the same 24
people/records already fixed in development_inputs_v1/selection_manifest.json.
Seal this selection and all input hashes before waveform reads. Verify each
WAV against official SHA256SUMS. Use its first min(6, duration) seconds through
the unchanged original frontend. Retain every frame; never decode the old
annotation-gap-collapsed training cache. Fail visibly on invalid records; no
silent deletion or replacement. Roles must be disjoint by linked person.

Teacher and student
Teacher: mean of the five frozen phase9 GRU teacher predictions. Verify all
checkpoint hashes and their existing inner-fit/validation group provenance.
This teacher was fitted on outer-training records; the 16 monitor people are
only excluded from NEW student fitting, not from teacher training. No OOF or
independent monitor claim. Targets use T=1 mean softmax logits for decoder
functions, and mean per-model T=2 softmax logits for emission KD.
Student: unchanged 4877-parameter PhysiologicalStudent, fresh random start.
Seeds: 20261113, 20261114, 20261115. Identical initialization and per-epoch
record order across arms within each seed. No data augmentation or crops.

Three arms, all retained
1. kl: mean per-record, per-frame KL(teacher_T2 || student_T2) times 2^2.
2. score: kl + 10 * smooth_L1(student_site_score, teacher_site_score, beta=.01).
3. query: kl + 10 * mean smooth_L1(G_student, G_teacher, beta=.01).
The site score is max murmur nominal confidence minus healthy confidence.
It is neither a calibrated diagnosis nor the quality-gated referral decision.
For query, use all four topologies, lambda=-1,+1, and three rewards: all frames,
first floor(T/2) frames, remaining frames. Normalize all rewards by FULL T.
Thus each record contributes 4*3*2=24 support-gap targets. No extra clipping,
projection pass or selection-aware constraint is included in these envelopes.

Consistent graph treatment
Both teacher and student construct their OWN duration potentials using the
unchanged original heart-rate and systolic-lag estimators on their current
T=1 posterior. These choose integer argmax lag indices; durations are locally
constant between discrete switches. Recompute student graphs on every forward
pass. Hold those discrete choices and resulting potentials fixed during the
selected-path derivative. This is a piecewise derivative, not differentiation
through argmax, nor a globally smooth objective. The student needs no teacher
at inference. Use the tested float64 legacy-boundary DAG for all path solves;
no bitwise float32 decoder equivalence is asserted.

Fixed training budget
20 epochs; batch size 8; Adam lr=.001; default betas/eps; no weight decay,
gradient clipping, early stopping, AMP or scheduler. Use final epoch only.
Record every epoch's training losses and monitor emission KL. Monitor values
never select a checkpoint/hyperparameter. Fail on nonfinite losses/gradients.
Save every final model, history, original initialization hash, data-order hash,
and all 24 development posteriors. No tuning after viewing results.

Evaluation and predeclared engineering gate
Development n=24 already-consumed people, one 6-second recording each. Report
each seed and arm, with per-record values. Primary bound error is the equal
average of (a) native global site-contrast lower/upper endpoint MAE and (b)
per-topology first/second-half reward lower/upper endpoint MAE, at eta=.005.
These measure fidelity to finite-query TEACHER OUTER bounds, not true extrema
or disease truth. Also report T=2 emission KL, nominal score MAE, and three-way
sign-state agreement (positive/nonpositive/unresolved) for eta .001,.005,.01,.05.
The same two queries are reused across eta; eta values are not replicates.

Pass only if query's primary mean error across three seeds is at least 10%
below BOTH kl and score, and query improves over BOTH controls in at least two
of three paired seeds, and its mean emission KL is no more than 5% above kl.
This is a descriptive engineering gate, not a p-value or clinical-superiority
criterion. Retain failures and tradeoffs. Even a pass requires stronger controls
(including structured-marginal KD), broader inputs and an unused confirmation
set before a new-method publication claim. No post-hoc favorable subset.

Implementation verification
Run selected-path value/gradient/CPU/CUDA tests after vectorization. Check
captured potentials against the prior saved diagnostic arrays. Verify teacher
and development inference denominators, provenance, finite gradients, matching
initialization/orders, and independently reconstruct final saved-posterior
metrics before interpreting the experiment. Preserve setup failures.
